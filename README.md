# Mental Health Outcomes by Social Media Use Among Students

## The data
4,500 students (high school to postgraduate), covering social media use, sleep,
GPA, stress and mental health.
Source: dataset by Obaidul Haque and Md. Samiul Alom, uploaded to Kaggle by
M. Khoirul Mustaqim.
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mkhoirulmustaqim/student-depression-detection-dataset

## Question
Is there a correlation between social media use and students'
academic performance and mental health?

## Findings
I found a negative correlation: as daily social media use goes up,
mental health and GPA go down.

- **GPA:** students using social media 7+ hours a day averaged a 3.00 GPA,
  compared with 3.73 for those using it under 3 hours.
- **Sleep:** heavy users (7+ hours) slept 5.4 hours a night on average,
  over 2 hours less than light users (7.7 hours).
- **Mental health:** the average mental health score fell from 91.9 to 62.7,
  and stress tripled (7.3 → 21.8).
- **What didn't matter:** platform and school level made almost no difference.
  How much students used social media mattered more than which app they used.
